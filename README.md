# koordinatesworld

> some things can't be explained. only encountered.

---

independent content studio. no institution. no agenda. no face.

writing at the intersection of fields that have no business being related —
and finding out they were always the same field.

---

**the origin story**

9th and 10th standard. everyone picks the safe electives.
picked advanced mathematics both years instead.

studied at Salt Brook Academy — the school in Assam that parents
fight to get their kids into. the kind where everyone around you
is performing at the ceiling.

11th standard. chose PCMB — physics, chemistry, maths, biology.
walked into the biology exam.
wrote nothing.
submitted a blank sheet.
walked out.

not a breakdown. not a protest.
just an honest answer to a subject that never asked anything interesting.

12th standard. still PCMB. biology still unread.

somehow that blank sheet led to a BSc in Biotechnology.
then an MSc in Geospatial Science and Geoinformatics from DTU Delhi.

biology to satellites.
the most interesting paths begin with a refusal.

---

**the operating system**

functions best alone.

not because people are the problem —
because silence is where the thinking actually happens.

osho made a distinction once:
loneliness is the absence of the other.
aloneness is the presence of oneself.

this work comes from the second kind.

---

**what happens here**

things that shouldn't connect — do.
questions that science won't ask — get asked.
answers that philosophy won't commit to — get tested.

read at your own risk.

---

**the work**

it exists. it goes deep.
find it when you're ready.

---

**who this is for**

people who got bored of being explained things they already half-knew.
people who prefer a long silence over a short answer.
